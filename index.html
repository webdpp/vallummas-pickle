Frequently Asked Questions

Details on shelf-life, shipping safety, storage tips, and ingredients.

Our pickles stay fresh for 6 to 9 months when stored properly. Since we do not use artificial chemical preservatives, keep the meat and seafood pieces submerged beneath the aromatic gingelly oil layer and always use a clean, dry spoon.
Each jar is fitted with an airtight inner foil induction seal and a leakproof cap. It is then shrink-wrapped, double-cushioned in thick bubble wrap, and packed inside a rigid corrugated box to withstand long-distance shipping across India.
• Kerala: 1 - 2 business days
• South India (Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad): 2 - 3 business days
• Rest of India (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, etc.): 3 - 5 business days via express courier with live tracking.
Strictly NO! We never add synthetic vinegar essences, MSG, class-II preservatives, or artificial red food color. The rich ruby-red color comes purely from premium Malabar and Kashmiri dried red chilies, roasted spices, and natural fermented coconut vinegar.
